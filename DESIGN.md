## Brand & Style

### Personality & Direction
This design system embodies a revered, meditative, and dignified digital Quranic sanctuary. The aesthetic balances classical Islamic heritage with modern, distraction-free craftsmanship. Its visual language evokes tranquility, sanctity, and refined elegance, deliberately steering clear of loud, hyper-technological interfaces in favor of warmth, reverence, and enduring balance.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for daily reciters, scholars, and thoughtful readers seeking prolonged devotional reading and deep study without visual fatigue. The interface should immediately evoke:
- **Sakina (Serenity):** Calm visual breathing room, subtle ambient surfaces, and restful organic tones.
- **Waqar (Dignity):** Impeccable classical calligraphic balance paired with bespoke gold-leaf accents.
- **Clarity & Focus:** Seamless interactions that honor the sacred text, elevating Quranic passages above UI clutter.

### Design Movement: Classical Spiritual Minimalist
A delicate fusion of high-end tactile bookbinding and contemporary Islamic minimalism:
- Gentle parchment backdrops simulating the tactile intimacy of natural deckle-edge paper.
- Precise, muted-gold geometric motifs used strictly as subtle architectural separators rather than loud ornamentation.
- Restrained glass and tonal surfaces that keep audio controls, tafsir drawers, and bookmarks weightless and respectful.

## Colors

### Color Strategy & Palette
The palette is built around sacred architectural tones: deep emerald green representing life, dignity, and sanctuary, complemented by an unpolished matte gold reminiscent of illuminated Quranic manuscripts.

- **Primary (`#0F382A` - Deep Emerald):** The foundational pillar for high-emphasis containers, active navigation states, primary call-to-actions, and noble headers.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` - Matte Islamic Gold):** Used for ayah markers, decorative micro-rules, active playback indicators, and sacred milestones. Provides warm illumination without blinding specular glare.
- **Tertiary (`#8C3A27` - Terracotta Carnelian):** A dedicated accent drawn from traditional bookbinder's seal wax, applied strictly for juz/hizb dividers, sajdah indicators, and cautionary prompts.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F2` - Warm Parchment):** The resting eye tone for prolonged daytime recitation, eliminating blue-light harshness. Supported by `#F4EFE6` for secondary cards and elevated containers.

### Dark Mode (Nocturnal Sanctuary)
- **Base Background:** `#0B1E17` (Deep Nocturnal Forest Green).
- **Surface Elevation:** `#132B22` for navigation bars, tafsir bottom sheets, and floating toolbars.
- **Text & Ayah Contrast:** `#E9E3D5` (Soft Alabaster) replacing pure white to prevent nocturnal irradiation, accented by luminous amber-gold (`#DFB76C`) for active ayah highlighting.

## Typography

### Typographic Hierarchy & Mechanics
The system utilizes a deliberate dual-font architecture:
1. **Sacred & Editorial Text:** Anchored by `Noto Serif` (with Amiri/Uthmanic Naskh runtime fallback), providing the organic pen-stroke variation and balanced ligatures necessary for Arabic vocalization and Quranic diacritics (tashkeel).
2. **Operational UI:** Structured with `Be Vietnam Pro`, delivering geometric balance, neutral modern counters, and high legibility across navigational labels, surah metadata, and timestamp telemetry.

### Line Height & Diacritic Safety
Quranic Arabic requires exaggerated vertical spacing. Ayah line heights (`54px` desktop / `46px` mobile) are calibrated to prevent collision between stacked sukuns, maddah signs, and ayah end-stops. Verse counters and tafsir annotations maintain independent, non-conflicting metrics.

## Layout & Spacing

### Reading Rhythms & Grid
The layout operates on a flexible 8pt structural rhythm, transitioning into a specialized page-proportion grid for sacred reading:
- **Mobile (Single Mushaf Page View):** Full-bleed reading canvas anchored with safe top margins for surah banners and a floating bottom bar for playback and chapter traversal. Padding uses `space-md` (`16px`) horizontal margins to avoid touch-clipping during edge swipes.
- **Tablet / Split View (Mus'haf + Tafsir):** Dual equal-pane or 60/40 golden-ratio distribution. Quranic text retains dedicated central margins (`space-xl`), isolating verses from the commentary drawer.
- **Desktop / Display:** Centered reader container with a fixed maximum text width of `760px` to guarantee an optimal 45–65 character reading line length.

## Elevation & Depth

### The Tactile Illuminated Manuscript Approach
Elevation in this system eschews cold, artificial dropshadows in favor of warm ambient diffuse lights and subtle tonal layering.

- **Level 0 (Parchment Foundation):** Flat `#FAF8F2` (Light) or `#0B1E17` (Dark). No elevation.
- **Level 1 (Ayah Highlight & Reading Tiles):** Subtle warm tone shift (`#F4EFE6` or `#132B22`), bounded by an ultra-thin 0.5px border of `#C5A059` at 25% opacity.
- **Level 2 (Audio Player & Floating Navigation):** Semi-translucent glass layered over parchment: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with a 90% opacity surface (`#FAF8F2EE` in light, `#0F382AEE` in dark), casting an ambient shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(15, 56, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Tafsir Overlays):** Deep ambient spread `0 16px 48px -8px rgba(11, 30, 23, 0.18)` paired with an inner top hairline border of matte gold (`#C5A059` at 40% opacity).

## Shapes

### Corner Curvature & Sacred Geometry
A balanced `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem / 8px base radius) is maintained to avoid the cold rigidity of sharp corners while preserving architectural dignity:
- **Surah Headers & Frame Borders:** Utilize 8px outer curves, complemented internally by delicate 8-point geometric star corner insets.
- **Audio Control Capsule:** Fully rounded pill shapes for the floating player dock (`rounded-xl` / 24px) to signal fluid touch interactions.
- **Ayah Verse End-Markers:** Symmetrical ornamental octagons holding gold-accented numeral typography.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Buttons:** Deep Emerald (`#0F382A`) background with Matte Gold (`#C5A059`) text and border outline (1px solid `#C5A05933`). Smooth scale transition (0.98) on press.
- **Secondary / Ghost Buttons:** Transparent fill with 1px border in `#C5A05966` and text in `#0F382A` (light) or `#FAF8F2` (dark).
- **Icon Actions:** 44x44px minimal tap target, circular hover halo in gold-tinted overlay (`#C5A05915`).

### Ayah Selection & Reading Row
- **Default State:** Transparent background, natural flow.
- **Active / Reciting State:** Soft amber-gold background wash (`#C5A05914`) with a 2px left-accent border in `#C5A059`, smooth fade transition (250ms ease-out).

### Floating Audio Dock
- **Container:** Elevated Level 2 capsule resting 16px above the viewport base.
- **Controls:** Play/Pause rendered as a prominent central gold disc (`#C5A059`) with an emerald icon (`#0F382A`). Scrub slider rendered in 3px slim track with emerald-to-gold gradient fill.
- **Telemetry:** Current reciter name in `label-md`, surah and ayah timing metrics in `label-sm`.

### Tafsir & Translation Bottom Sheet
- **Container:** Swipeable modal anchored at bottom with 16px top border radius.
- **Typography:** Dual-tier arrangement featuring the original ayah in `body-quran` separated by a subtle geometric divider, followed by scholastic commentary in `body-tafsir`.

### Chips, Pills & Navigation Tags
- **Juz / Hizb Markers:** Compact capsules with parchment surface (`#F4EFE6`), gold border (`#C5A0594D`), and emerald micro-labels.
- **Audio Speed / Reciter Selectors:** Tonal toggle chips with smooth 200ms background cross-fade.

### Search & Index Inputs
- **Input Fields:** Recessed parchment containers (`#F4EFE6`) with soft inner shadow, 1px matte gold focus ring, and custom right-aligned calligraphic magnifying glass icon.
